Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

Fontify ("we", "our", "the site") is a free online text transformation tool. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Text Input

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any text you enter. All text transformations happen entirely in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your text never leaves your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how our site is used. This collects anonymized data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral source

Google Analytics does not collect personally identifiable information. You can opt out by using a browser extension like Google Analytics Opt-out or by enabling Do Not Track in your browser settings.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Theme preference — Stores your dark/light mode choice (localStorage)
  • Google Analytics — Standard GA4 cookies for analytics purposes

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — For traffic analysis (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts — For typography (the Inter font family)
  • Netlify — For hosting and content delivery

Advertising

We may display advertisements in the future. If we do, ads will be served by reputable ad networks that comply with applicable privacy regulations. This policy will be updated to reflect any advertising partners.

Children's Privacy

Fontify does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The site is a general-purpose text tool suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
