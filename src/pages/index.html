About Fontify — Free Fancy Text Generator

Fontify is a free online tool that lets you convert plain text into stylish Unicode fonts and symbols. Unlike regular text formatting which requires specific app support, Unicode text works everywhere — social media bios, messaging apps, emails, documents, and more.

How Does It Work?

Unicode includes thousands of characters beyond the standard alphabet. Many of these look like styled versions of regular letters — bold, italic, cursive, gothic, and many more. Fontify maps your text to these alternate Unicode characters, creating styled text that you can copy and paste anywhere without installing fonts or apps.

Where Can I Use These Fonts?

The styled text works on Instagram bios and captions, Twitter/X posts and bios, Discord messages and usernames, Facebook posts and comments, WhatsApp messages, TikTok bios, YouTube comments, LinkedIn profiles, and any other platform that supports Unicode text.
