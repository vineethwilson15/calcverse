About CalcVerse

CalcVerse is a free collection of online calculators and unit converters. Every tool is designed to be fast, accurate, and easy to use on any device.

How It Works

All calculations happen right in your browser — nothing is sent to a server. Your data stays on your device. Always.

What We Offer

  • Financial Calculators — EMI, SIP, compound interest, loan, and more
  • Health Calculators — BMI, age calculator
  • Math Tools — Percentage, discount calculator
  • Unit Converters — Length, weight, temperature, speed, area, volume, data, and time
  • Everyday Tools — Tip calculator, GST calculator

Open Source

CalcVerse is open source. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no dependencies. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.
